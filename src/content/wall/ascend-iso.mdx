  Promise kept: Harnest is now ISO 27001 certified!

  Our clients trust us with their most sensitive information: their extra-financial data, their steering indicators, their strategic roadmaps.

  This certification confirms that we protect it with the level of rigor it deserves.

  Concretely, what does it mean?

  ISO 27001 is the international reference standard for information security. It attests that we have put in place a rigorous system to identify cyber threats, manage risks and guarantee the confidentiality, availability and integrity of our clients' data.

  To put things in perspective: in France, barely 1,700 organizations of all sizes and sectors hold this certification.

  We are one of them.
