  We received our SOC 2 Type II report on December 31st.

  There is no better way to close out the year and kick off 2026 than with a big stamp of approval that our security and data practices are sound.

  At Bond, we are building an AI Chief of Staff. When you hire for that role, you are trusting that person with the unfiltered reality of your business. That trust is not a nice to have. It is the product.

  Security is a journey that does not begin or end with a piece of paper. It is a mentality.

  That is why we did not wait for a compliance deadline to start caring. We have been scanning our code and infrastructure with tools like Aikido Security since our first few weeks of development, and we partnered with Probo early on to ensure our compliance processes were just as robust as our tech.

  Because of that groundwork, achieving this milestone took just 4 months after I joined as CTO. We did not have to scramble to build a security posture. We just had to document the one we already had.

  The report is fresh for the new year. The security is standard.
