The thirdParty vet operation is now available in the Probo n8n node.

Use it to kick off a third-party vetting job from any workflow — onboarding automation, vendor intake forms, periodic reviews. The operation queues the job and returns immediately. The vetting report runs asynchronously in Probo.

No more blocking your workflow while waiting for an AI analysis to complete.
