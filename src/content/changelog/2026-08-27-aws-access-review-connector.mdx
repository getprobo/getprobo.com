Most access review connectors need a token or key pasted into Probo. The new AWS connector skips that step. Probo mints a short-lived identity assertion, and your AWS account exchanges it for temporary credentials against a role you control.

Connect once and every account under that AWS organization is tracked automatically as a cloud account, no per-account setup required.

Nothing to rotate, nothing to leak. If you ever want to cut access, you just remove the role on your side.
