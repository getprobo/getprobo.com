After the SOA moved to the document publish workflow last week, data and asset registries were the odd ones out. Still exports. Still snapshots. Still a separate workflow to explain to auditors.

Not anymore. Both registries publish like documents now. Version them, approve them, sign them, archive them. One workflow across every compliance artifact in Probo instead of three.
