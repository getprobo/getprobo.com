description: "Portal visitors can request access to several locked documents at once instead of one at a time."
date: 2026-07-27

Visitors to your compliance portal can now select several locked documents, reports, or files and request access to all of them in one call.

Before, that meant clicking request access on each document, one by one. If a prospect wants your SOC 2 report, your latest pentest, and a couple of policies before a call, they should not have to file five separate requests to get there.
