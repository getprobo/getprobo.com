This release adds Google Analytics (GA4), Dotfile, Segment, Square, and UpCloud to Access Reviews.

Each connector pulls in account listings and resolves roles and statuses on its own, and shows up with its own logo in the source picker. Add one, run a campaign, done.

That brings the connector list to well over a dozen in the past couple months. If a tool you use for access reviews still isn't on it, tell us and we will add it.
