## What is Probo?

Probo is an open-source compliance management platform that helps organizations achieve and maintain compliance across multiple frameworks — including SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 42001, GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, FERPA, CASA, NIS2, and DORA. Built for startups and growing companies, Probo provides risk management, evidence collection, third-party management, policy management, and audit preparation tools.
